# Third-party material

Rust dependencies remain under the licenses recorded by their upstream packages;
Cargo.lock pins the resolved dependency versions. Swift Apple frameworks are
platform-provided dependencies, not redistributed frameworks. Optional Core ML
experiments depend on coremltools and NumPy under their own licenses.

MIT applies to the original project code and owned model weights. It does not
relicense human game data, game-related artwork, or upstream dependencies.
The project maintainer confirmed human corpus redistribution for community reuse
on 2026-09-29; provenance is retained. See data/README.md.
Website artwork, account profiles, raw tournament participant metadata, and
private deployment infrastructure are not included.
